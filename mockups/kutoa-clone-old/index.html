Our Projects

Kenya

Pendo Child & Family Development Hub

Opened January 2026—already serving 300 children, with special education, a computer lab, dance & wellness, and teacher training. Future capacity: 700.

  • Early childhood education
  • Family & child wellness
  • Digital literacy lab
  • Teacher training
  • Community hub space

Canada

Pendo Sisters

No mother should wait alone—supporting refugee mothers separated from their children through peer community, wellbeing, and long-distance parenting.

  • Peer & community support
  • Maternal wellbeing & retreats
  • Preserving mother–child bonds
  • Long-distance parenting
  • Reunification pathways

We align our work with the UNSDGs

Goal 4: Quality Education — Target 4.2. Ensure that all girls and boys have access to quality early childhood development, care and pre-primary education so that they are ready for primary education.

Why we do this work

Pendo International Projects is rooted in lived experience—building pathways of dignity for children and mothers across Kenya and Canada. We believe every child deserves a bright beginning, and every mother deserves a community that holds her up. Read our story.
