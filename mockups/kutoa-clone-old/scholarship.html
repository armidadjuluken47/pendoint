How the scholarship works

Built for clarity: what is covered, what full and half mean, and how families partner with the school.

What sponsorship covers

Scholarships are focused so families and donors know exactly what is included.

  • Tuition — school fees for the child’s place
  • Food — meals provided through the school
Important: Sponsorship caters for tuition and food only. Uniforms, transport, and other costs are not included unless arranged separately.
Type

Full

Full scholarship support for the child’s tuition and food for the agreed period.

Type

Half

Shared support—part of tuition and food is covered so the family and sponsor share the cost.

Family partnership

Scholarships are a partnership. Parents stay involved in school life, and we treat media consent as its own clear choice.

Volunteer Saturdays

Parents who receive sponsorship agree to volunteer at Riverside Murera School two days each month—the first and third Saturday—supporting the community that supports their child.

Photos & stories

With permission, the school may share a child’s photos or videos on school channels to celebrate learning and invite support. Families can discuss this when applying; it should never feel like a condition buried in fine print.
