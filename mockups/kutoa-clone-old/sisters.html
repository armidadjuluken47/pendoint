Countless

Mothers face isolation, anxiety, and depression—while mother–child bonds weaken across distance and time.

Refugee mothers flee to Canada for safety—often forced to leave children behind. Pathways for reunification exist, but almost no support holds mothers through the wait.

Pendo Sisters exists to change this.

No mother should wait alone.

Every year, refugee mothers arrive in Canada while their children remain overseas. Prolonged separation harms maternal wellbeing and the bond that shapes a child’s development.

Through peer community, mental health support, and long-distance parenting tools, Pendo Sisters walks with mothers so the wait does not break them—or the bond with their children.

“I lived this separation for more than eight years. There were no programs to support mothers like me. Pendo Sisters was created to fill that gap.”

— Ciro Muiruri, Founder & Executive Director

How to join

Pendo Sisters is for refugee mothers in Canada navigating prolonged separation from their children. Reaching out is confidential and without pressure.

Who it’s for

  • Refugee or newcomer mothers living in Canada
  • Mothers separated from children who remain overseas
  • Anyone seeking peer support, wellbeing care, or long-distance parenting tools during the wait for reunification
Your privacy matters Conversations and participation are confidential. We never require public stories or photos to receive support.

What happens next

  1. 1Reach out by email or our contact form and tell us you’re interested in Pendo Sisters.
  2. 2We’ll respond personally, learn a little about your situation, and answer questions.
  3. 3If it’s a fit, we’ll welcome you into peer community, wellbeing support, and parenting tools at a pace that feels safe.

Founder’s story

Ciro Muiruri

Founder & Executive Director, Pendo International Projects. Speaker, advocate, and author.

Ciro arrived in Canada as a refugee in 2016. Her story was featured in Toronto Life’s “Brave Heart,” which led to a book offer from House of Anansi Press. Her forthcoming memoir chronicles prolonged mother–child separation, resettlement, and community leadership.

In Canada, Pendo Sisters is inspired by Ciro’s own eight-year separation from her child—peer support, mental health services, and long-distance parenting tools so no mother waits alone.

“I lived this separation for more than eight years. There were no programs to support mothers like me. Pendo Sisters was created to fill that gap.”

— Ciro Muiruri

Questions & Answers

Why was Pendo Sisters created?

Refugee mothers often spend several years separated from their children, with no programs supporting their mental health or helping them preserve the mother–child bond. Ciro lived this experience and built the program to fill this gap.

What makes Pendo Sisters unique?

Pendo Sisters is the only program in Canada focused specifically on maternal wellbeing during prolonged separation.

How long are mothers typically separated from their children?

Immigration backlogs can mean mothers and children are separated for several years. In Ciro’s case, it took eight years for her to be reunited with her daughter.

What does the program offer?

Pendo Sisters offers peer support, mental health programs, wellness retreats, long-distance parenting tools, and reunification preparation.

How many mothers have been supported so far?

More than 150 mothers in the pilot phase.

How can people support the program?

Through partnerships, funding, referrals, and amplifying awareness. Reach out at ciro@pendointl.org or visit www.pendointl.org.
