The Problem in Numbers

200

Children filled the old school to capacity—many more families could not be accommodated.

<6%

Of primary schools in Kenya have computer labs. Digital literacy was out of reach for most children.

0

Special education units existed nearby. Children with disabilities had nowhere local to learn and belong.

Crowded classrooms, missing special education, and almost no digital access left children without the start they deserve.

Pendo built the Hub to change this.

Every child deserves the best start in life.

Pendo School began in 2019 on a small piece of land Ciro inherited from her late father. Completing the first and second floors of the Hub marked a turning point—driving enrollment, team growth, and new programs for children, families, and educators.

Through early childhood education, inclusion, digital literacy, wellbeing, and teacher training, the Hub is transforming what is possible for children and families in Ruiru.

“Watching children walk through the doors of the Pendo Child & Family Development Hub for the first time reminded me why we started this journey.”

— Ciro Muiruri, Founder & Executive Director

How the Hub makes a difference

The Hub created more than classroom space. It unlocked special education, digital learning, mental health programming, and a centre for teacher training.

01 · Inclusion

Community’s first Special Education Unit

Partner: Jade, Brazil · 15 children in specialized support

Individualized instruction, specialized teaching, speech and language therapy, occupational and physiotherapy, assistive technology, and IEPs—so every child is valued and included.

  • 5 iPads + assistive learning software
  • Ongoing technical support and resources
02 · Digital

Computer lab & digital literacy

Partner: TechLit Africa

Less than 6% of primary schools in Kenya have computer labs. At Pendo, students gain regular access to computers, coding, and foundational digital skills for an increasingly digital world.

03 · Wellbeing

Child & youth mental health

Partner: ChezaCheza Dance Foundation, Kenya · 400+ reached weekly

The Pendo Dance & Wellness Initiative combines dance movement therapy, social-emotional learning, and mindfulness—open to community children every Saturday and during school holidays.

  • Mentorship · dance therapy · counselling
  • Nutritious meals · parent engagement
04 · Educators

A centre for teacher training

Partner: Nesting Play, Hungary · 25 teachers

Nesting Play’s team travelled to Kenya for professional development with Pendo educators and teachers from neighbouring schools—raising the standard of early childhood education across the wider community.

Our journey

2019–20

Daycare beginnings

Pendo begins as a small daycare serving six children on land inherited from Ciro’s father—then weathers COVID by keeping every staff member employed and delivering learning materials door-to-door by motorbike.

2020–23

Steady growth

Demand grows; limited classroom space means many families cannot be accommodated. The vision for a purpose-built Hub takes shape.

2023–26

Hub construction

With partners including 11:11 Foundation ($75,000 CAD across 2023, 2025, 2026) and others, floors 1 and 2 are completed.

Jan 2026

Pendo moves in

After two years of fundraising and construction, Pendo School moves into the Hub. Within two weeks, enrollment rises from 200 to 300—the largest increase in Pendo’s history.

Looking ahead

The exterior of the four-storey Hub is complete, but floors 3 and 4 remain unfinished. These are our most urgent priorities.

Urgent

Running water

Reliable water for classrooms, meals, hygiene, and daily Hub operations.

Urgent

Kitchen

A proper kitchen to sustain nutritious meals for growing enrollment.

Priority

Completed classrooms

Finish flooring and interior works on remaining floors for child-friendly learning spaces.

Priority

Safe outdoor play

Playgrounds and recreation areas where children can explore, exercise, and learn through play.

Priority

School bus

Safe, reliable transport so more children can reach Pendo and community programs.

Vision

Floors 3 & 4

Completing the upper floors unlocks the Hub’s full 700-child capacity.
