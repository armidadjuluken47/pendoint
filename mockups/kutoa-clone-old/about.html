About Us

We Ignite!

Limitless potential in every child.

Pendo International Projects exists to make high-quality, affordable early childhood education accessible—and to walk with families toward stronger futures in Kenya and Canada.

Our founding story

Rooted in lived experience

Ciro Muiruri is the Founder of Pendo International Projects. Born in Kenya’s Kawangware community, orphaned by AIDS as a child, she experienced firsthand the barriers marginalized children face in accessing affordable, high-quality education.

Her tenacity carried her through those inequalities. She became an early childhood educator, community developer, and social entrepreneur—establishing schools and later opening a Montessori school near Nairobi.

In 2015, after surviving violence and her daughter Naima’s abduction, Ciro escaped to Canada as a refugee and rebuilt her life. Driven by love for children and a vision for accessible early learning, she founded Pendo’s first initiative: Pendo School Kenya, which opened to learners in 2019.

“Pendo School is dedicated to Naima: stolen but not forgotten.” — Ciro

Our Work

We break systemic barriers to early childhood education. In Kenya, ECE remains largely informal and underfunded—especially in rural and low-income communities. Pendo School is our flagship response: a community-based model of quality and affordability, with a vision to equip more schools through training, mentorship, and shared tools for success.

Pendo Pedagogy

We teach Kenya’s National Curriculum, blended with Indigenous knowledge rooted in local culture—so learning reflects children’s reality.

Food & Nutrition

Two healthy school meals a day, sourced from local farmers, prepared on-site with nutritional balance and food-safety standards.

WASH

Clean water, sanitation, and hygiene are part of daily routine and curriculum—essential to every child’s well-being.

The Right to Play

Creative play, sports, games, and free play help children engage in learning and gain critical life skills.
