Our School

Your child’s best start

We believe every child is loved, and deserves a bright beginning.

Pendo International Projects is a Canadian registered charity operating Pendo School Kenya—high-quality, affordable early childhood education for children under seven.

Our approach is rooted in excellence, dignity, and community: families pay affordable fees; donations keep quality within reach.

Explore our work

Building hope since 2018

Changing lives and building hope through early education.

From breaking ground in 2018 to a growing campus and a new Child & Family Development Hub, Pendo School Kenya models what quality and affordability can look like together.

We train teachers, mentor community educators, and walk with families—because the earliest years shape everything that follows.

Let’s talk about our impact

From four classrooms to a campus built for the next generation.

Since 2018 we’ve broken ground, welcomed our first class, graduated learners, expanded classrooms, purchased land, and opened a new multi-storey campus and Child & Family Development Hub.

We align our work with UNSDG Goal 4—quality early childhood development, care, and pre-primary education for every child.
