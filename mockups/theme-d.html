Who we are

We believe every child deserves the best start in life.

We increase access to affordable, high-quality early childhood care and education in Kenya’s low-income communities.

Play, nutritious meals, WASH, and the Kenya National Curriculum—blended with care that helps parents know exactly what they’re choosing.
