# Homepage — Squarespace section blueprint

Build each row as a **Fluid Engine** section. Suggested layout types are Squarespace block names.

---

## Section 1 — Hero (full width)

**Layout:** 1 column, centered (or image background + text overlay)

| Element | Content |
|---------|---------|
| H1 | A high-quality early childhood school—and a mission you can support. |
| Body | Pendo School Kenya is a high-quality, affordable early childhood school operated by Pendo International Projects, a Canadian registered charity. Families pay affordable school fees. Donations and sponsorships help keep those fees accessible and support nutritious meals, teacher training, facilities, and the wider mission. |
| Button 1 | **Explore our school** → `/our-school` |
| Button 2 | **Support our work** → `/donate` |

*Kutoa parallel: big mission statement + two early questions (“who we are” / “need to talk”) — here: parents vs supporters.*
