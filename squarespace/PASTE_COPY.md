## PENDO SISTERS (`/pendo-sisters`)

**SEO title:** Pendo Sisters | A program of Pendo International Projects  
**SEO description:** Supporting maternal wellbeing and preserving the mother-child bond for refugee mothers in Canada.

**H1:** Pendo Sisters  
**Tagline:** No Mother Should Wait Alone  
**Sub:** Supporting Maternal Wellbeing. Preserving the Mother-Child Bond.  
**Line:** A program of Pendo International Projects

### The crisis
Refugee mothers in Canada are often separated from their children overseas. Separation that should be short can last years because of immigration backlogs. Prolonged separation can bring isolation, anxiety, and depression—and can strain the mother–child bond.

### Our solution (4 columns or summaries)
1. **Peer & community support** — Virtual group calls, gatherings, retreats—a community that understands.  
2. **Maternal wellbeing** — Mental health programs, professional expertise, wellness retreats.  
3. **Preserving mother–child bonds** — Daily practices that strengthen attachment and connection.  
4. **Long-distance parenting & reunification preparation** — Practical guidance for parenting from a distance.

### Why Pendo Sisters
Few programs focus on mothers during years of separation. Pendo Sisters walks alongside refugee mothers during the long years apart from their children—reducing isolation and supporting wellbeing while strengthening the bond for future reunification.

**Founder note:** Built on lived experience—our founder, Ciro Muiruri, navigated more than eight years of prolonged mother–child separation, revealing a critical gap in support.
