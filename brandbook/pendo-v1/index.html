Brand Overview

This is the strategic foundation of our guidelines. It defines what Pendo International Projects is and what we stand for—so every visual and verbal decision stays purposeful and consistent across Kenya and Canada.

Who we are

P is for Pendo

Pendo International Projects is a Canadian registered charity advancing high-quality, affordable early childhood education in Kenya and maternal well-being for refugee mothers in Canada.

We increase access to care and learning rooted in dignity, community, and every child’s potential—through Pendo School Kenya, the Child & Family Development Hub, and Pendo Sisters.

This brand book is the master blueprint for visual identity in digital and print.

Brand Logo

The logo section defines every allowed version, size, spacing and prohibition. It protects the single most visible mark of Pendo—so it always appears clear, consistent, and trustworthy.

Logo & wordmark

Free space

We use a primary logo mark with the Pendo name. Keep clear space around the lockup equal to at least the height of the mark’s inner emblem.

On light backgrounds use the full-colour logo. On navy or photography, use a white / reversed treatment. Never crowd the mark with other logos or dense type.

Typography

Our system pairs Classico for display with Montserrat for body and Oswald for wide labels—clarity, warmth, and a strong brand voice across media.

Primary display

Classico

Classico is the primary display face for Pendo—used for headlines, section titles, and hero statements. It stands in for the refined heading voice of our Kutoa-inspired system.

Body and UI use Montserrat. Navigation, buttons, and eyebrows use Oswald (wide caps).

Fallback: Optima / Georgia for Classico · Helvetica Neue / Arial for sans

04

Brand Colour

Colour carries trust, warmth, and clarity. Navy anchors the brand; teal signals care and growth; ember highlights action and hope.

Colour usage

Navy leads.
Teal & ember support.

Use navy for primary fields, headlines, and footer chrome. Teal for accents, links, and labels. Ember for CTAs, active states, and emphasis rules.

Foam / marble neutrals keep layouts breathable—especially behind photography and long reading text.
